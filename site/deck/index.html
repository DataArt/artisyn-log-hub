You already know what AI coding costs — per month, per day, per session — and controlling that is right and useful. But a number alone is not an answer: this session cost {{ds:session.total|usd0}}. Is that a lot or a little, good or bad? How do you make it cheaper — or when is it worth spending more for better quality? Log Hub answers the next questions: why it costs that much, which factors drive it, whether your harness works the way you designed it, and what your tools really bring — for one session and across the team. The example: a team of {{ds:team.developers}} developers, {{ds:daily.sessions}} sessions a day, {{ds:team.workingDays}} working days — {{ds:monthly.total|usd}} a month. All figures are fictional.

Context growth, not output, drives the bill.

  • Every turn re-reads the whole context.
  • One large file or Bash output inflates every turn after it.
  • Context Growth drills down to the step that did it.

Self-hosted: Docker and Postgres, a one-command install; your data stays in-house. The hook installs into a repository via npx and captures the full session transcript by design — see Data collection in the docs. Backward compatible: a new server keeps accepting uploads from older hooks. Sessions can be exported and imported between Log Hub installations.
